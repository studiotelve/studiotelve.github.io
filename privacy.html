Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sleuth Match is made by Studio Telve. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

We do not collect personal data ourselves. The game has no account and no analytics of its own. Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device and never sent to us.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed; we never see your name, card or payment details. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to those payments.

Ads are optional: a video plays only when you choose to watch one for a reward. These videos come from Google AdMob. To show ads and count them, Google may process device information such as an advertising identifier, your IP address and how you interact with the ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other apps. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the game removes everything it saved. Your rights under laws such as the GDPR and KVKK remain; for any question, write to us.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
